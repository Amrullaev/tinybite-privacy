Barchinoy Obidova ("Developer", "we", "us") built the Kichkina Luqma app ("App") as a free assistant for caring for small children. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. The App does not send any user data over the internet.

All app-related data (settings, reminder schedules, usage history) is stored only locally on your device and can be permanently removed at any time by uninstalling the App.

2. Device Permissions

The App uses the following system features:

  • Local notifications — to send you reminders about feeding, medication, or other care events. These notifications are created entirely on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Internet access — only used to load in-app images and animations. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Local storage — to save your settings and reminders on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any advertising, analytics, or user tracking third-party services. No ads, no tracking.

4. Data Security

Because all data is stored only on your device, access to it depends entirely on you. We do not store any data on our servers, so your information is protected from external threats.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for parents and guardians. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. In fact, the App does not collect any user information at all, so children's data is equally safe.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We advise you to review this page periodically.
